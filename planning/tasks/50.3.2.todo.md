## Notes
- Analysis shows `corridor_scoring.py` had duplicate evaluators (`ZeroedCriterionEvaluator`, `FarmSizeCriterionEvaluator`, `StandardCriterionEvaluator`) and delegated Criterion 2 to `PerimeterCriterionStrategy` in `lesa.py`. Unifying them under a flexible `CriterionEvaluator` hierarchy eliminates duplication while preserving both constructor signatures (`crit_num` only and `crit_num, max_points, name`).
- `CriterionEvaluation` subclasses `SiteAssessmentCriterion`, providing full compatibility with `tests/test_corridor_scoring.py` and `SiteAssessmentSummary`/`CorridorSiteAssessmentSummary`.
- Downward-only dependencies strictly honored: imports only from `core.constants`, `models.lesa`, and `domain.site_assessment`. No imports from `clients/`, `spatial/`, `mcp/`, or root flat files.
- Existing root files (`src/fpac_soil_mcp/lesa.py` and `src/fpac_soil_mcp/corridor_scoring.py`) remain untouched as required by task scope.
