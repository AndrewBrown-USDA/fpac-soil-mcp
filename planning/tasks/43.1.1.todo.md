# Plan 43.1.1 — Define verified Criterion 2 scoring contract

## Scope

Define the normalized Criterion 2 perimeter-agricultural-adjacency contract
for site and corridor assessments, preserving preliminary-screening authority
boundaries and compatibility with the unified LESA models.

## Notes

- ADR 0003 requires preliminary framing and prohibits presenting the result as
  an official FPPA determination, LESA score, or agency-approved assessment.
- ADR 0007 keeps shared LESA architecture in `lesa.py` and preserves
  project-type-specific Site Assessment limits.
- Existing constants define Criterion 2 as 10 points for both site and
  corridor projects; do not alter unrelated criteria or LE normalization.
- Do not infer agricultural use from “not urban”; unavailable upstream evidence
  must remain explicit and must not be converted to zero.
- No live-network test is required for this contract-only task; live TIGERweb
  behavior belongs to task 43.1.2/43.3.1.
