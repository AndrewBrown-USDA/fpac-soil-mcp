## Notes
- ArcGIS REST polygon geometry specification dictates that exterior rings are clockwise (`LinearRing.is_ccw is False`) and interior hole rings are counter-clockwise (`LinearRing.is_ccw is True`).
- `_polygon_to_esri_rings` already handles the inverse transformation (Polygon/MultiPolygon -> ArcGIS rings with CW/CCW normalization).
- Constraints honored: Files in scope are strictly `src/fpac_soil_mcp/tigerweb_client.py` and `tests/test_tigerweb_client.py`.
