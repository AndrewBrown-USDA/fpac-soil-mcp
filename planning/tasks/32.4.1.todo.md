# Task 32.4.1 Micro-Plan — Expand Benchmark and Regression Coverage

## Scope

Extend benchmark and regression coverage for all four validated productivity
selectors (NCCPI, CSR2, CPI, and STORIE), including scale-aware assertions,
sentinel handling, selector propagation, and the Storie raw-then-final rounding
contract. Do not change production routing or SQL. Do not implement until this
plan is approved.

- `tests\fixtures\le_benchmarks.json`
- `tests\test_lesa.py`
- `tests\test_polygon_analysis.py`
- `tests\test_corridor_analysis.py`
- `tests\test_sda_client.py`
