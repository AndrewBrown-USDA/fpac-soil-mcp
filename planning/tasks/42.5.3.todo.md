# 42.5.3 -- Frontend: AOI in sessionStorage, sent with each turn

## Goal
The map AOI is attached to the open chat, kept in sessionStorage per chat id, shown as a chip, and sent with every chat turn as `custom_inputs.active_aoi`. The "Add result to chat" button and summary prefill are gone.
