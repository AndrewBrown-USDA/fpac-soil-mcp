# 56.4.1 -- E: keep geometry context through stream retry

## Goal
The `generateText` fallback after a failed stream still sends `active_aoi` and `active_corridor`, even if the client disconnected first; both keys are released once the stream and any fallback finish, on every path.

## Why the bug is plausible
`pipeUIMessageStreamToResponse` tees the stream into `streamCache` (resume), so `execute` keeps running after the client disconnects. `res.on('close')` releases both keys then, and `fallbackToGenerateText` -> `databricksFetch` finds nothing in the registry.
