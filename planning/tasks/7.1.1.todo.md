## Notes
- Unit conversions: 1 foot = 0.3048 m, 1 mile = 1609.344 m, 1 meter = 1.0 m.
- EPSG:6933 (NSIDC EASE-Grid 2.0 Global) is an equal-area cylindrical projection suitable for global/national geodesic area and buffering calculations without planar distortion.
- Pyproj `Transformer.from_crs("EPSG:4326", "EPSG:6933", always_xy=True)` enables high-precision projection round-trips.
