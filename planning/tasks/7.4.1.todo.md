## Notes
- 7 CFR § 658.5(c) explicitly modifies the 12 site assessment criteria for corridor projects: criteria 5 and 6 are assigned 0 points, while criteria 8 and 11 are increased from 10/15 points to 25 points maximum each, preserving the 160-point SA total.
- Combining composite LE (0-100) and SA (0-160) gives a max score of 260 points. If Total >= 160, project requires assessment of alternatives.
- Must preserve preliminary screening disclaimers.
