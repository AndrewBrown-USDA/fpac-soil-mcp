## Notes
- TIGERweb County endpoint: `https://tigerweb.geo.census.gov/arcgis/rest/services/TIGERweb/State_County/MapServer/1/query`.
- Attributes: `GEOID` (5-digit FIPS: 2-digit state + 3-digit county), `NAME` (e.g. "Story County" or "Story"), `STATE` (2-digit state FIPS).
- Geometries returned as ESRI rings; parsed into Shapely Polygon/MultiPolygon.
