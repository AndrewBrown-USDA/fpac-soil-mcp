# 56.6.1 -- B1: chat-scoped AOI/corridor context

## Goal
While no chat is open (e.g. "Loading chat..." between chats), the map cannot write AOI or corridor context, and nothing is written to the previous chat's storage; each chat keeps its own AOI and corridor.

## Approach
Two layers: providers ignore writes when `chatId` is null (catches every path, incl. chip detach and Use-as-AOI), and `MapPanel` disables the inputs so users see why.

## Discovered (out of scope)
- Cold start, more evidence: every single-test Playwright run (3 of 3) failed at its first map-ready wait, because that test is always the first load on a fresh Vite server. Earlier probes: a normal cold load takes about 5 s (the expect default) and about 1 in 5 cold loads never fire MapLibre `load` until reloaded. Proposed fix: a Playwright warm-up (globalSetup) that opens the fixture, waits up to 30 s, and reloads if not ready.
- Suspected, not verified: returning to a chat with an unattached corridor draft re-attaches it under a new `corridor_ref` (the attach signature resets on switch). Item C (56.8.1) territory; the new test compares the centerline, not the ref.
