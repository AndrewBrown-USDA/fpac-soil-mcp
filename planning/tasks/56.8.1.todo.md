# 56.8.1 -- C: Undo / Clear / Detach symmetry

## Goal
Undo restores the previous AOI exactly (same `aoi_ref`, geometry, summary); Clear and chip Detach are undoable; Undo is disabled with nothing to restore. Corridor: chip Detach goes through `clearCorridor`.

## Decision (user, 2026-11-17)
Keep Undo AOI-only: no corridor Undo (TRIAGE_56 C-corridor option b).

## Approach
Move the one-level AOI history into `ActiveAoiContext` via a pure `aoiHistory.ts` (Node-testable). Every AOI change (draw, paste, Use-as-AOI, Clear, chip Detach, cross-detach by a corridor attach) saves the previous full context; `undo` restores it as-is. History is per chat (dropped on chat switch) and in memory only.
